# ADR 0002: Bitterroot has no humans

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-26

## Decision

All people in Bitterroot are anthropomorphic or half-beast animal people.
Humans do not exist in history, settlements, crowds, travel, player defaults,
or generated incidental cast.

## Enforcement

- Mandatory world lore states the rule.
- Final roleplay instructions repeat it.
- Character data requires an animal species.
- Regression tests inspect both canon and compiled prompts.
